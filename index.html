02 / MANIFESTO • FOUNDER PHILOSOPHY

An aquarium is not a decorative container—it is a closed hydrological architecture requiring disciplined biological filtration, balanced illumination, and uncompromising hardware.

Founded by Amit Kumar in Swatantra Nagar, Narela, Delhi, SwimWithUs Aquatics was established to bridge the gap between discerning Indian fishkeepers and world-standard aquarium hardware. Too many hobbyists struggle with mismatched pumps, misleading flow rates, and unverified components that collapse under continuous operation.

We treat aquatic equipment with engineering rigor. Every filter series, hanging WRGB luminaire, quartz heater, and volcanic substrate in our 240+ product catalogue is vetted for long-term electrical safety, quiet operation, and true biological performance. We serve customers across India with insured shipping and direct WhatsApp sizing guidance.

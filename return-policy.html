STORE POLICY • RETURNS & REPLACEMENTS

Return & Refund Policy

Fair, transparent guidelines for product damage, functional defects, replacements, and order cancellations.

1. Transit Damage & Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) Policy

While we take exceptional precautions to pack delicate items securely, transit damage may occasionally occur. If your item arrives broken or cracked, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery.

Mandatory Requirement: To facilitate prompt courier claims and immediate replacement dispatch, we require a clear, uninterrupted unboxing video filmed from the moment the outer courier packaging and seal are opened until the product is examined.

2. Eligible Replacement Scenarios

  • Physical Transit Damage: Cracked glass heaters, damaged filter bodies, or cracked light fixtures supported by an unboxing video.
  • Defective Components: Filter motors or pumps that fail to start during initial dry or bench testing.
  • Incorrect Item Received: If the model, flow rate, or size delivered differs from your confirmed WhatsApp order invoice.

3. Non-Returnable Items

For biosecurity and hygiene reasons, the following categories are strictly non-returnable once opened:

  • Open containers of fish foods, supplements, or water conditioner liquids.
  • Substrates, ADA soils, or sands once opened or placed into water.
  • Aquarium medications or chemical treatments.
  • Products damaged due to dry-running of pumps, electrical surges, or user negligence.

4. Replacement Procedure

  1. Notify founder Amit Kumar on WhatsApp at +91 95604 48232 within 24 hours of receiving the parcel.
  2. Share your unboxing video, photographs of the courier shipping label, and description of the defect.
  3. Upon verification, a replacement unit is booked and dispatched within 24 to 48 hours at no additional shipping fee to you.

5. Refunds

If an exact replacement is unavailable due to inventory depletion, we issue a 100% full refund directly to your original payment method (UPI / Bank Transfer) within 2 to 3 business days.

6. Order Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled free of charge at any time prior to courier dispatch. Once a parcel has been handed over to Delhivery, DTDC, or India Post and an AWB has been generated, cancellations cannot be processed until the parcel is returned.

7. Support Contact

For any return or replacement query, please message Amit Kumar on WhatsApp at +91 95604 48232 or email swimwithusaquatics@gmail.com.

STORE POLICY • TRANSIT & PACKAGING

Shipping Policy

Transparent dispatch timelines, transit couriers, and specialized protective packaging standards for all aquarium equipment across India.

1. Nationwide Pan India Delivery

SwimWithUs Aquatics ships aquarium products, accessories, filter media, lights, and foods to hobbyists and commercial aquarists throughout India. All consignments originate from our central dispatch facility located in Swatantra Nagar, Narela, Delhi – 110040.

2. Order Processing & Dispatch Timeline

  • Standard Dispatch: In-stock orders are processed, quality-inspected, and dispatched within 24 to 48 business hours following WhatsApp confirmation.
  • Order Verification: Because aquarium equipment must properly match tank sizes and electrical specs, our founder Amit Kumar verifies order details via WhatsApp prior to boxing.
  • Operating Days: Dispatches occur Monday through Saturday. Orders placed on Sundays or gazetted public holidays are processed on the subsequent working day.

3. Courier Partners & Delivery Estimates

We partner with dependable Indian logistics providers including Delhivery, DTDC, Blue Dart, Professional Couriers, and India Post Speed Post for remote locations.

  • Delhi NCR & North India: 2 to 4 business days.
  • Metro Cities (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Chennai, Hyderabad): 3 to 5 business days.
  • Rest of India & Tier 2/3 Towns: 4 to 7 business days depending on pincode accessibility.

4. Specialized Shockproof Packaging

Aquarium electronics, quartz glass submersible heaters, delicate impeller assemblies, and fluorescent/LED lighting fixtures require extraordinary care. We use multi-layer bubble wrap, reinforced polystyrene corner bumpers, and corrugated outer cartons sealed with security tape.

5. Tracking Your Shipment

Upon package dispatch, the tracking airway bill (AWB) number and direct carrier tracking link are shared via WhatsApp and email. You can monitor your shipment's live transit milestones directly from the courier portal.

6. Shipping Charges

Shipping rates are computed based on parcel dead-weight and volumetric dimensions. When you submit your shopping cart or enquire on WhatsApp, Amit Kumar will calculate and share the exact logistics charges upfront before you confirm payment.

7. Delivery Queries

For questions regarding delivery coverage to your specific pincode or to request urgent express delivery, please message us directly on WhatsApp at +91 95604 48232 or email swimwithusaquatics@gmail.com.

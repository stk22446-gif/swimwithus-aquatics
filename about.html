About SwimWithUs Aquatics

Dedicated to supporting aquarium enthusiasts, hobbyists, and professional aquascapers across India with verified hardware and dependable advice.

Welcome to SwimWithUs Aquatics

Founded and managed by Amit Kumar, SwimWithUs Aquatics is an Indian aquarium specialty supplier based in Swatantra Nagar, Narela, Delhi. We started with a clear, singular vision: to make high-quality, genuine aquarium equipment, filtration technology, LED lighting, and aquascaping materials easily accessible and reliably delivered to fishkeepers throughout India.

Our Philosophy: Authentic Hardware for Thriving Aquariums

An aquarium is a living, breathing ecosystem. Whether maintaining a desktop planted nano tank with show bettas or managing a 500-liter monster community aquarium, water quality, circulation, and reliable temperature control determine the health and longevity of your aquatic life.

At SwimWithUs Aquatics, we believe hobbyists should never have to gamble on counterfeit or subpar equipment. We work directly with established manufacturers and verified importers such as SOBO, SUNSUN, Minjiang, Seachem, ADA, SISO, Bluepet, and API to bring genuine products to your doorstep.

Comprehensive Product Catalogue

  • Advanced Filtration Systems: Top sump filters, slim hang-on-back units, quiet internal power filters, dual sponge filters, and multi-stage external canister filters with integrated UV clarifiers.
